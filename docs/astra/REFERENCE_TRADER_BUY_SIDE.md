# BUY-side evidence and limits

Knowledge version: tradingcartel-breakfast-v1.

All eight user-supplied execution videos reviewed in this pass first enter SELL. Public Sep6/Sep10/Sep22 also first sell. Public Sep26 provides actual BUY-history losses and a retrospective explanation of two buy ideas. The trader therefore trades buys, but the successful live BUY first-entry/addition process is not comparably established by this corpus.

BUY symmetry is **derived symmetry pending equivalent empirical evidence**. A candidate mirrored model would examine a strong fall into a relevant low/demand area, inability to accept lower prices, bullish response or failed bearish reclaim, then current fresh bullish control. Add only to a profitable owned BUY campaign after renewed proof; do not rescue losing buys. A clean directional mirror is an engineering hypothesis, not evidence that the trader uses identical thresholds or timing.

The Sep26 lesson is independently useful: a demand-zone label is attention, not sufficient confirmation; continued downward structure can defeat it. The first narrated buy is described as confirmed but failed. The second is admitted as rushed without final confirmation. Prevailing-sell-trend diagnosis is retrospective; the video does not prove it was correctly recognized before either trade and deliberately ignored.

Until comparable prospective BUY clips/ticks exist, track BUY and SELL separately in retrieval, evaluation and outcomes. Never report symmetric empirical coverage or a BUY win-rate estimate from SELL examples. New social research is linked through the evidence index and must retain the same provenance rules.
