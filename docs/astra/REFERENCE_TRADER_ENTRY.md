# First-entry recognition

Knowledge version: tradingcartel-breakfast-v1. Desired decisions are research labels based on the visible prefix, not labels generated from later profitability.

## Readiness questions

1. Identify the attempted move and the relevant pre-existing reaction area.
2. Identify specific failure evidence and any contrary acceptance still present.
3. Identify the recent proof: opposing displacement, failed recovery, retest rejection, or renewed continuation.
4. Identify the current entry location and the next opposing reaction area.
5. Distinguish fresh local opportunity from the same old story after its useful move is spent.
6. State a concrete contradiction and abstain when the visible prefix does not specify permission.

'Why now' means a current change in control or an executable failure/retest, not merely a higher score. It does not imply an exact universal candle-close requirement. A fresh continuation can remain actionable after substantial distance from the old peak; an apparently fresh candle can also appear at a poor location near opposing structure.

## Correcting historical overstatements

GROK_ENTRY_AUDIT_2026-09-09.md and APEX_V384_ENTRY_RETEST.md assert a universal displacement-origin retest and describe missed non-retest runners as the trader's actual universal choice. The larger original/public corpus contradicts that certainty. Their synthetic architecture tests remain useful implementation evidence, but cannot establish a universal human entry law. Existing docs/REFERENCE_TRADER_BREAKFAST_SETUP.md also overstates proximity to the original extreme as universal; public Sep6 and NEW clip8 require a local-event interpretation.

No fixed maximum distance from the old high or wait of N closes has been learned. Future labels must record current local origin, actual quote, counterevidence and residual room. An unsupported anticipatory example remains unsupported even when it later wins.
