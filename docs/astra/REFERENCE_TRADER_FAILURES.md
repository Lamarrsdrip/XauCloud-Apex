## Required negative fixtures

Synthetic controls must be clearly marked synthetic: sweep without failure→WAIT; isolated wick under persistent upside→WAIT; old response with spent local room→WAIT; extreme/local invalidation reclaimed→INVALID; negative basket despite desired direction→STOP_ADDING; prior failed idea with no new setup→WAIT; repeated event/decision→no new order. None may be counted as a real trader loss or as performance evidence.

Use chronological prefix labels. Record later outcomes separately; source examples are correlated campaigns, not independent tickets. Freeze labels before holdout returns. Do not infer reliability, win rate or account-flip probability from the promoted sample.
