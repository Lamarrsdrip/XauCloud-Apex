# Profit-first: measurement rather than a promise

Knowledge version: tradingcartel-breakfast-v1.

The selected examples frequently produce favorable reactions and scale before a large Gold move. They also show initial negatives and later losses. A sell opens at bid and closes/marks at ask; buys use ask then bid. Spread alone can create red P/L. Do not teach 'never red' or infer a bad setup solely from a briefly negative mark.

NEW clip8 gives a particularly clear counterexample: SELL2 at4178.353; chart P/L -18.00 at60s, -32.20 at62s, account -26.00 at66s (ask4178.483), +1.00 at68s, -38.40 at70s, account -19.00 at72s, +45.60 at74s, account+140.60 at78s. The displayed spread is about0.24 in those charts. These are snapshots, not full extrema. Original clip3 shows -9.35 at56s before later positives. Original clip4 briefly shows+0.04 at44s then-0.50 at46s. Neither monotonic immediate profit nor universal favorable-before-adverse is established.

On causal broker ticks, fix the fill and entry-time ATR. For a sell E, favorable=max(0,E−ask(t)), adverse=max(0,ask(t)−E); for a buy use bid(t)−E and E−bid(t). Add commission/swap/currency conversion separately. Record raw favorable bid/ask movement, first spread-adjusted green, first meaningful favorable threshold, and first adverse threshold with censoring and tie policy. Candle high/low does not give intrabar ordering.

Measure at10s,30s,60s,180s and300s plus full hold: MFE, MAE, MFE-before-MAE at predeclared0.25/0.5/1.0 entry-ATR thresholds, latency from local proof, distance from current origin, first/second add movements, positions before1/3/5/10 Gold, and immediate adverse campaign counts. Keep L1 and each add separate. Repeat the same stream/settings for Lifestyle Native versus Astra; do not use edited-video elapsed time as market-time latency.

These video sources cannot supply the requested exact10-second/30-second/minute population statistics, because of cuts, missing bid/ask paths and changing phone clocks. Such results remain unavailable until native replay or prospective logging provides them. Displayed favorable lower bounds are descriptive only.
