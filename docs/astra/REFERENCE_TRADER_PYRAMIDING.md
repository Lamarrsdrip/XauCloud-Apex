## An add decision must name its market event

The campaign is already favorable after execution costs. Seller pressure continues, a recent counter-push fails, a lower high holds, a local shelf breaks, or price accepts lower prices after a pullback. The add has its own local origin and contradiction. Re-evaluate remaining room and extension from this proof, not just accumulated movement from L1. Current positive basket P/L is necessary for the requested no-rescue policy; it is not sufficient proof of opportunity.

Do not require every existing ticket to be green: spread and a normal pullback can make the newest ticket negative. Do not require each additional fill to be strictly lower than the last fill as a universal trader rule: clip3's4054.086 follows4054.080 and NEW clip8's4177.188 follows4177.070 while the broad campaign remains favorable. The native Lifestyle ladder may retain its own executable spacing; any change must be independently evaluated and clearly distinguished from evidence.

## Distinct proof versus batches

Several examples contain repeated same-price fills without a separately visible structural event per ticket. That is one proof followed by a batch or rapid execution sequence, not proof of many independent opportunities. A production integration must consume each decision/snapshot/event once. A repeated model response must not create repeated layers. If batching is implemented, request it explicitly, bound the total executable exposure deterministically and reconcile actual fills. This knowledge version does not prescribe a new batch size.

## When to stop adding

Stop when the new proof loses force, current location is spent/poor, price repairs the local failed structure, quote/decision becomes stale, basket is non-positive, or thesis is invalid. A large old MFE and unused margin are not permission. clip7's later2-lot sells near4342.938 then rebound are an essential counterexample: good L1 plus green historical basket does not make every later add good. Freeze additions before deciding whether the entire broad thesis is invalid; these are different decisions.
